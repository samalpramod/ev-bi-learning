# Business KPIs

| KPI                       | Business Question                             |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Total Revenue             | How much money did we generate?               |
| Total Transactions        | How many charging sessions occurred?          |
| Energy Consumed           | How much energy was delivered?                |
| Average Transaction Value | What is the average revenue per session?      |
| Active Charge Points      | How many charge points are operational?       |
| Utilization %             | How effectively are charge points being used? |
| Revenue Growth %          | Is the business growing?                                 |
| Total active Operators    | How many active operators in the system       |
| Average transaction/day   | How many transactions per day                 |
| Total Vehicles            | How many vehicles recharged                   |
| Average revenue/vehicle   | What is the average revenue per vehicle       |
